# Credits

ngoi borrows ideas and some text from these projects. All are MIT licensed.

- **superpowers** by Jesse Vincent (https://github.com/obra/superpowers, v7.0.0). `skills/systematic-debugging/SKILL.md` is adapted from its systematic-debugging skill. Ideas: the builder check on a spec, implementer status reports, a bounded final review round, and letting the fixer reject a finding.
- **pstack**, Copyright (c) 2026 Lauren Tan (https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack). `skills/unslop/references/wording.md` adapts patterns from its unslop skill. Ideas: proving changes on the real app, a per-repo verification guide, the same review prompt across models with lead judgment, scaling review to the change, attack the premise, the scrap rule, and the principles. `tldr` takes its shape from the ketchup and recall skills: assume the user remembers only their own message, word budgets, and a status on every thread.
- **GSD Core** (https://github.com/open-gsd/gsd-core). Ideas: verifying backwards from the goal, "presence is not behavior", the evidence gate for findings, scope-reduction checks, gray areas before planning, and listing how verifiers go soft.
